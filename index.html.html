Where nature finds its next chapter

Our story

Mulungu began with a simple observation. The branches carefully pruned from a garden were never empty of purpose, only waiting for another.

Today, we honour that belief by transforming reclaimed natural materials into functional art, preserving their character while revealing new possibilities. Because nothing in nature is without value.

Workshop photo placeholder
Our purpose is to reconnect people with nature through thoughtful design, creating pieces that bring warmth, meaning and a sense of belonging into everyday life.
